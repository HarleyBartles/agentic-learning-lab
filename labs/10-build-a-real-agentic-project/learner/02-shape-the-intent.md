# 2 — Shape the intent

Open a local worker in your new project repository.

Describe what you roughly want, but do **not** ask it to build yet.

A useful prompt is:

> I want to make [your rough idea]. Do not build it yet. Propose a small first version: what it is for, how it should be structured, what content or features it needs, the visual or interaction direction where relevant, assumptions you are making, and anything you think I should decide before implementation.

Read the proposal as a person who owns the outcome, not as an implementer.

Change whatever you dislike.

You can say things like:

- that sounds too formal;
- remove that section;
- give me three alternatives;
- keep that idea but change the tone;
- I have nothing useful to put there, suggest something else;
- this matters more than that;
- make the first version smaller.

You do not need to translate those judgments into code or detailed technical instructions.

Keep refining until the proposal is good enough to try.

The loop so far is:

```text
you describe intent
        ↓
agent proposes
        ↓
you judge and refine
```

When you are willing to see that proposal become a real first version, move to the next card.
