# 3 — Build and inspect

Tell the worker to build the first version from the proposal you approved.

Ask it to review its own result before handing it back.

Then inspect the artifact yourself.

For the default website, preview it locally and open it in a browser.

Do not reduce your review to `I like it` or `I don't like it`.

Ask yourself:

- Does it communicate what I wanted?
- What is clearly strong?
- What is clearly weak?
- What feels merely acceptable?
- Is important information easy to find?
- Do the main links or interactions work?
- What happens at a narrower viewport?

You do not need design or programming vocabulary.

`The text feels cramped`, `I don't know where to look first`, or `this sounds too generic` are useful observations.

The first version may already be good. You do not need to invent a failure.

The point is to treat the result as something you can inspect and judge rather than something the agent has declared finished.

Write down one meaningful weakness you would actually like improved.

Then move to the next card.
