# Field constraints

- The reserve is open to the public, so the team must not leave equipment unattended.
- No mains power is available at the sensor site.
- The team should avoid entering marked nesting areas.
- The mast should not be climbed without the portable fall-arrest kit.
- The sensor enclosure requires a 4 mm hex key.
- The local data card must be recovered even if the sensor cannot be repaired.
- Mobile reception is unreliable beyond the visitor centre.
