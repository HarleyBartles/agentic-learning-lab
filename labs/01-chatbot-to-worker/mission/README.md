# Field Sensor Recovery

## Mission

A wildlife monitoring sensor at the North Fen reserve has stopped reporting. A two-person team needs a short recovery brief before travelling to the site.

The project information for this mission is in `source/`.

Produce the finished mission brief at:

`output/mission-brief.md`

## Mission brief requirements

The brief must:

- state the recovery objective;
- identify the **current** access route and timing;
- list the equipment the team should take;
- capture the important operational constraints;
- identify the main unresolved field risk;
- apply later information that supersedes earlier plans;
- avoid presenting superseded and current instructions as unresolved alternatives.

## Win condition

The mission is complete when `output/mission-brief.md` exists locally and accurately reflects the current project state in the source material.

A plausible brief based on an obsolete plan does not satisfy the mission.
