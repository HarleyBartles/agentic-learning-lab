# Field plan

A wildlife monitoring sensor at the North Fen reserve stopped reporting overnight.

Send a two-person team to inspect the unit, recover its local data card, and restore reporting if the fault can be fixed safely on site.

## Access and timing

Meet at the east gate at 09:00 and follow the service track to the sensor mast.

The service-track walk is normally about 20 minutes each way. Allow up to 90 minutes at the mast for inspection, data-card recovery, and any safe on-site repair.

Plan to be back at the vehicle by 15:30. The visitor centre closes at 16:30.

## Equipment available for the visit

- replacement sensor battery;
- spare local data card;
- basic first-aid kit;
- waterproof map case;
- handheld GPS;
- portable fall-arrest kit;
- 4 mm hex key.

## Field priorities

Recover the local data card even if the sensor cannot be repaired.

Avoid unnecessary disturbance to the nesting area and do not enter marked nesting areas.
