# 03 — Don't mark your own homework

The worker has reported completion and told you how it checked itself.

Now verify the claim independently.

The claim is:

> **Every current visitor-facing output uses the approved programme name, and only the intended current-output surfaces changed.**

Use simple evidence:

- search `output/` for the old name;
- confirm the approved name appears where expected;
- inspect the diff;
- inspect which paths changed;
- read the changed visitor-facing copy for meaning, not just string replacement;
- confirm historical evidence and archive material were not rewritten.

One important trap:

> Finding the old name somewhere in the project does **not** automatically prove failure.

Historical evidence and archived copy are allowed to preserve old wording honestly.

Verification must match the claim you are actually trying to establish.

If the evidence is good, accept the work.

If it is not, give the failed evidence back to the worker, let it repair the bounded defect, and verify again.

Earn:

> **A completion message is a claim. Verification is the evidence that lets the human accept or reject that claim.**

And:

> **The agent should check its own work. The agent does not get to mark its own homework.**
