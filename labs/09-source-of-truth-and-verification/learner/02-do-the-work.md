# 02 — Do the work

Now that you know what defines correct, authorize the change.

Ask the worker:

> Bring every current visitor-facing output into line with the approved public programme name. Do not alter the authority map, current visitor brief, preserved evidence, or archive material. Review your own work before you tell me it is done, and tell me what evidence you used.

Let the worker perform the task and self-review it.

Do not accept the completion message yet.

A strong worker may have done everything correctly. That is fine.

The lesson is not `agents are unreliable`.

The lesson is:

> **A completion message is a claim.**

The next question is what evidence would make you willing to accept that claim.
