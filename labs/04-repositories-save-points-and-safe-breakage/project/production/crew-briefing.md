# Crew briefing — Northstar

## Arrival

All crew should enter through **Stage Door** and sign in with production management.

Crew call is **09:00**.

If you are bringing equipment, check `access-and-load-in.md` before arrival because vehicle access uses a different route from pedestrian entry.

## First hour

- check in at Stage Door;
- collect a radio if your role requires one;
- confirm your work area with the department lead;
- report access problems to production management;
- keep the loading-bay route clear once unloading is complete.

## Venue basics

Rivermill Theatre has limited backstage circulation space. Cases should not be left in the Stage Door corridor.

Tea, coffee, and water are available in the Green Room.
