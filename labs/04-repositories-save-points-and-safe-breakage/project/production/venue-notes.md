# Venue notes — Rivermill Theatre

## Access

Pedestrian crew entry is through **Stage Door**.

The loading bay is on Mill Lane and should be used only for equipment unloading. Vehicles must clear the bay promptly after unloading.

## Backstage

Backstage circulation is narrow around Stage Door and the crossover. Do not store flight cases in either route.

The Green Room is the main crew welfare space and the location for the 09:10 production-management briefing.

## Practical notes

- production office: first floor, stage-left stair;
- Green Room: ground floor, opposite Stage Door corridor;
- loading bay: Mill Lane side of the building;
- crew sign-in: Stage Door desk;
- venue contact during setup: house manager via production management.

Some of this information is repeated in crew-facing documents because those files need to be usable independently during the day.
