# Access and load-in — Northstar

## Load-in

Load-in begins at **08:00**.

Equipment vehicles should approach via Mill Lane and use the loading bay.

The loading bay must remain available for short unloading windows. Do not leave vehicles parked there after unloading.

## Crew arrival

Crew arriving on foot should use **Stage Door**.

Crew who unload equipment at the loading bay should move their vehicle away from the bay and then report to Stage Door to sign in.

## Restrictions

- keep the Stage Door corridor clear;
- keep the crossover clear;
- do not leave cases in fire routes;
- coordinate unusually large deliveries with production management before arrival.

## Escalation

If access is blocked or a delivery cannot be handled safely, stop and contact production management rather than improvising a new route through public areas.
