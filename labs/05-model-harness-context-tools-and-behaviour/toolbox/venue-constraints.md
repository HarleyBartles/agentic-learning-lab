# Riverside Centre venue constraints

## Foyer

The foyer may be used for reception and welcome desks.

Any desk must leave the marked 1.5 metre accessible exit route clear at all times.

## Studio B

Studio B is fitted with sensitive smoke detection.

Soldering, hot-work, naked flames, smoke effects, and other heat-producing demonstrations are not allowed in Studio B.

Cold demonstrations and ordinary seated workshops are allowed.
