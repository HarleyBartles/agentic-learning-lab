# Volunteer schedule constraints

The event uses two shifts:

- 18:00–20:00
- 20:00–22:00

Each shift must have at least two volunteers at Welcome and at least two volunteers at Repair.

No volunteer can cover two stations during the same shift.

Availability and station restrictions:

- Sam cannot work the 20:00–22:00 shift.

- Lee cannot work at Repair.
