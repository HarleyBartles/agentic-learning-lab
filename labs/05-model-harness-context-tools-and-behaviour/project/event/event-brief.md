# Riverside Makers Evening

Date: Thursday 24 September 2026

Public hours: 19:00–21:30

Volunteer arrival: 17:45

Doors open: 18:30

Venue: Workshop Hall, Riverside Centre

Expected audience: approximately 80 people

The event has three activity zones:

- repair table;
- demonstration stage;
- quiet help desk.

Operational constraints:

- no food or drink is allowed in the workshop area;
- the demonstration stage needs a 10-minute reset between live demonstrations;
- the accessible route is through the east entrance and must remain clear;
- visitors under 16 must remain with an adult.

The volunteer lead should confirm the east entrance route is clear before doors open and check that each activity zone has an assigned volunteer.
