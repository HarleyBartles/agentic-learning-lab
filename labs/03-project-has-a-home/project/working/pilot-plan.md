# Repair Café pilot plan

## Purpose

Run a friendly three-hour Repair Café pilot where local people can bring one small broken item and work with volunteers to diagnose or repair it where practical.

## Confirmed operating plan

- Riverside Community Hall.
- Saturday public session, 09:30-12:30.
- Free entry.
- One item per visitor.
- Four volunteers covering electricals, sewing, bicycles/general mechanical work, front desk/triage, and refreshments.
- No petrol-powered equipment, large appliances, or work that creates significant fumes, dust, or fire risk.
- Visitors should be involved in the repair where practical.

## Arrival model

Undecided.

The pilot still needs to choose between drop-in attendance, booked time slots, or a hybrid approach.

The decision should balance:

- keeping the event informal and welcoming;
- avoiding a large queue at opening time;
- giving visitors some confidence about waiting time;
- making attendance workable for people who benefit from a guaranteed slot.

## Communications still needed

Once the arrival model is decided, update the public-facing plan so visitors know:

- whether they need to book;
- whether they can simply turn up;
- what happens if the event is busy;
- what accessibility arrangements are available.
