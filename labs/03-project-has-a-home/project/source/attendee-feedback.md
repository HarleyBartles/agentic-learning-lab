# Prospective attendee feedback

A few prospective visitors were asked what would make the pilot easier to attend.

Common themes:

- Several people liked the idea of simply dropping in because they would not know exactly when they could get away from family or shopping.
- Two people said they would be put off by having to book a precise appointment for something meant to feel informal.
- Several people were worried about arriving and then waiting a long time with no idea when they would be seen.
- One visitor who uses mobility aids said knowing there was a guaranteed slot would make attendance much easier.
- Another visitor said they would happily book if it prevented everyone arriving at opening time.
- A few people said they would prefer to browse, have tea, and talk to other visitors while waiting rather than treating the event like a service desk.

There is no clear consensus from the feedback about whether the pilot should be drop-in, booked, or some mixture of the two.
