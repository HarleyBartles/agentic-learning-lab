# Volunteer notes

Four volunteers have confirmed for the pilot.

- Alex: small electrical appliances, fault finding, soldering, cable and plug replacement.
- Priya: sewing, clothing repairs, zips, hems, and basic fabric patching.
- Martin: bicycles, mechanical adjustments, hand tools, and general fixes.
- Jo: front desk, triage, visitor coordination, simple repairs, and refreshments.

All four can attend for the full public session.

The volunteers would prefer a steady flow rather than a large queue forming at opening time.

Alex and Priya are comfortable working with visitors alongside them rather than taking items away to repair privately.

The group wants the pilot to feel welcoming and informal rather than like a formal appointment service.
