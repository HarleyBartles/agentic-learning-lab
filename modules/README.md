# Planning modules

Files in `modules/` are facilitator planning material for curriculum that has not yet been promoted into mature labs.

## Numbering policy

Curriculum numbering is **course-local**.

- Course 1: Labs 1–10.
- Course 2: starts again at Lab/Module 1.
- Course 3: starts again at Lab/Module 1.

Older root-level planning drafts with numeric prefixes such as `11-`, `12-`, `14a-`, or `18-` preserve the historical order in which the curriculum was originally sketched. Those prefixes are no longer authoritative curriculum numbers.

Use the course-specific planning index when referring to current material:

- [`course-2/README.md`](course-2/README.md) — authoritative Course 2 planning sequence.

Do not rename or rewrite old planning drafts merely to erase their history. When a draft is substantively revised or promoted into a mature lab, give the new current artifact its course-local number.
